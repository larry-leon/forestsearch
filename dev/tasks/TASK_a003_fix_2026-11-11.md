# TASK — A-003 fix: specificity and NPV when no subgroup is found, 2026-11-11

Complete on its own. Run from the forestsearch repo root. Authorized by Larry 2026-11-11.

## Purpose

- **Kind of change: changes behaviour. It does not change the method.**
- In `R/run_simulation_analysis.R`, a replicate that identifies no subgroup under an
  alternative stores `spec` = |Hc|/N and `npv` = 1. The two values are in each other's
  column.
- With no subgroup found, the estimated complement is everyone. By the definitions in
  León et al. (2024), Section 3, and by this file's own found-case formulas
  (`tn / (tn + fp)`, `tn / (tn + fn)`), the values are `spec` = 1 and `npv` = |Hc|/N.
- Evidence: `dev/reports/REPORT_audit_revisit_2026-11-11.md`, section A-003.
- This task swaps the two values at the four sites, adds a test, and nothing else.
