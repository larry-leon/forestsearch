## Why

`vignettes/articles/` holds seven tracked `.html` files: earlier standalone renders of
articles, made 2–9 November with an older package. During a site build, an article that links
to a sibling page makes Quarto copy the sibling's old `.html` into the site, and pkgdown then
skips the fresh page ("not generated by pkgdown"). The site render task hit this for
`mrct-consistency-simulations` (`dev/reports/REPORT_site_render_0.5.0_2026-11-13.md`, note 1).
With no rendered `.html` beside the sources, it cannot happen.

- **No `R CMD check`, no test suite.** Nothing in the CRAN tarball changes (Step 4 proves it).
- **Work on `master`.** If the checkout is on `cran-prep`, switch it to `master`. Do not merge.
- **Move, never delete.** No `.html` render is deleted or edited.
- No article source, no `R/`, `man/`, `tests/`, `DESCRIPTION`, `NAMESPACE` or `_pkgdown.yml`
  change. No `.gitignore` or `.Rbuildignore` change.
- Commits are path-limited. **No push, pull, merge or tag.**
- No install into the user or site library. Scratch: `/tmp/site051`.
