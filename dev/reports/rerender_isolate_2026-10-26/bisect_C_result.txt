fix(mr): admit on the screen's rounded consistency threshold

MR's consistency floor used qnorm((1 + p_star)/2), the exact cutoff, while
the search admits on round(Pcons, pconsistency.digits) >= p_star. Build the
floor from .fs_pcons_eff(p_star, digits) instead. fs_mr_inference() gains

passes its own. Forest search only: GRF/DINA carry no consistency floor.
