### paragraph beginning 'Read together'
Read together: the median draw retains 66% of the region’s members, a
tenth of the draws retain 0% or less, and 2% of draws contain all of it.
Exact re-selection is rare; what moves from draw to draw is the
boundary. These diagnostics measure re-selection within the fixed
candidate family under multiplier perturbation; the bootstrap’s and the
cross-validation’s recovery rates (forestsearch_bootstrap_dofuture(),
forestsearch_Kfold()) measure re-discovery from resampled data with the
family rebuilt, which is stronger and costs a full re-run each time.

The multiplier correction is seconds; the bootstrap is minutes at the B
its intervals need, and the ratio widens with B. The seconds are those
of this machine and this package version: the MR column is the whole
multiplier stage as the fit records it (timing_seconds), the bootstrap
column the whole forestsearch_bootstrap_dofuture() call. The
manuscript’s figures — 72 s to about 10 minutes at B = 1,000 on 11
workers, 0.1 to 0.4 s for the multiplier stage — were recorded under
forestsearch 0.2.0 on another machine, and are not comparable second for
second; the order of magnitude between the two routes is the point.
