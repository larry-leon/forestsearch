Where they differ

1. Selected rule — 2 of 12 detected replicates

sim_id 17 — cosmetic, not a real disagreement.
new {size <= 21} & {meno} vs old {size <= 21} & !{meno == 0}. Logically the same set for a binary: identical n_harm (80), sens, spec, naive (1.9280) and β(Ê) (0.6104). Only the label index moved (q28.1 → q30.0), which means the candidate-cut enumeration changed between package versions.

sim_id 15 — a genuine selection difference.
new !{size <= 27} & {size <= 30} — n_harm 74, sens 0.21, naive 1.4445, β(Ê) 0.7132
old !{size <= 27} & {age <= 53} — n_harm 123, sens 0.54, naive 1.3071, β(Ê) 0.7013
The new run picked the narrower, higher-effect region (1.4445 > 1.3071) — the signature of sg_focus = "maxeffCons" (highest effect meeting the consistency rate) against a consistency-primary focus, which favours the larger, more stable region.

FB moves by 0.2% with mixed sign across replicates — consistent with RNG-stream ordering, not a change in the procedure. MR moves by ~10% in one direction on 9 of 10 replicates, and its IJ SE is larger on 10 of 10. That is a change in the MR implementation, not resampling noise. The likely mechanism is documented in the setup chunk itself: MR’s de-biasing rule is no longer set explicitly — forestsearch() now derives it from sg_focus, so a different focus yields a different re-selection family.

Caveats

  • The old bundle cannot confirm its own configuration. Its meta has no sg_focus, focus_tag, consistency_method, selection_rule or package version — those provenance fields postdate it. The fs_t1_t2_ stem predates focus-in-stem, which is precisely the condition the template warns about: runs under different foci silently shared one file. The focus attribution above is inference from behaviour, not a record.
  • Two causes are confounded. The sim_id 17 label shift proves the cut enumeration changed between versions, so sim_id 15 could be a focus difference or a re-enumeration tie-break. Re-running the old configuration under the current package would separate them.
  • Timing is not comparable. fit_mr_secs (4.7 s) is the whole forestsearch fit including MR; t2_secs (15.7 s) may be MR alone. FB 132 s vs 113 s reflects different hardware/topology.
  • Sim 1–20 is a mild low outlier of the 500. Detection 60% here vs 69.2% across all 500; mean old-MR 0.955 vs 1.003 over the full pool. Conclusions about agreement hold regardless, but do not read the 20-rep aggregates as the cell’s operating characteristics.
