## Framing

p̂(Ĥ) is a **recorded diagnostic**, computed from draws already made after the bounds are formed; **no construction in this package reads it, and nothing in this task proposes that any should.** The fixed-p̂ bands below are a re-cut of committed simulation results for analysis only — the same replicates, grouped by a stratum definition that means the same thing at every n and prevalence, because the per-cell tertiles used so far shift their boundaries with n (12.4% T3 is [0.268, 0.991] at n = 500 and [0.594, 0.996] at n = 1500) and therefore cannot answer an n-comparison. Reading p̂ as an analysis-time flag (high p̂ ⇒ the stable-pick regime, bounds somewhat optimistic) is the existing documented rule and is unchanged. p̂ is estimated from the same draws that build the bound, so it is not an independent instrument — adequate for a flag and for diagnostics, and a reason not to let any interval depend on it.

## G4 — Reading (Part G)

**The miss is one-sided, and the persistent-bias mechanism is supported.** Wherever the harm block loses two-sided coverage the interval lies entirely *below* β(Ĥ) — the de-biased estimate is too low relative to the realized target, not too high. At 12.4% HR 1.75 the IJ two-term's miss-below runs 0.0332 / 0.0759 / 0.0836 against a miss-above of 0.0058 / 0.0075 / 0.0035; the field's runs 0.1026 / 0.1151 / 0.1026 against 0.0100 / 0.0347 / 0.0190. The bias does not shrink with n while the SE does: IJ bias −0.0101 / −0.0806 / −0.0929 log units (it *grows* in magnitude) against mean SE 0.3929 / 0.3015 / 0.2576, giving bias/SE −0.026 / −0.267 / −0.361; the field's bias is −0.0748 / −0.0911 / −0.0750 against mean SE 0.3633 / 0.2802 / 0.2313, bias/SE −0.206 / −0.325 / −0.324.

**Persistent bias alone is not sufficient — the SE's cushion over the error SD is the other half.** At 31% HR 1.75 bias/SE grows the same way (IJ −0.105 / −0.197 / −0.254) and two-sided coverage does not decay at all (0.9720 / 0.9740 / 0.9805). What differs is SE / error-SD: 1.125 / 1.092 / 1.221 at 31% against 1.034 / 0.933 / 1.045 at 12.4%. At 31% the IJ SE overstates the error spread by 9–22% and absorbs the bias; at 12.4% it is at or below the error SD and there is nothing to absorb it with. The same contrast holds on the field, whose SE / error-SD is 0.983 / 0.953 / 1.003 at 31% HR 1.75 (two-sided 0.911 / 0.915 / 0.930, low and flat) against 0.910 / 0.826 / 0.887 at 12.4% (0.887 / 0.850 / 0.878). The decay is the joint condition — bias/SE growing while SE/error-SD sits at or below 1 — not either factor alone.

**The decay is stratum-localized, and localized at the opposite pole from the stable-pick caveat.** At 12.4% HR 1.75 n1500 the IJ two-sided runs, low p̂ to high, 0.857 / 0.591 / 0.734 / 0.827 / 0.925 / 0.987; at n500 the same six bands run 0.975 / 0.942 / 0.943 / 0.977 / 0.978 / 0.965. ## H4 — Reading (Part H)

**Detection conditioning is second-order; it contributes nothing measurable to the harm-block bias trajectory or to the two-sided decay.** Restricting each series to its always-detected `sim_id` set moves every quantity in H2 by at most 0.0038 (the largest change anywhere in the table, on 31% HR 1.00 n500 complement field-s). The decay itself is untouched: at 12.4% HR 1.75 the IJ two-sided runs 0.9611 / 0.9166 / 0.9129 on all detected and 0.9608 / 0.9147 / 0.9147 on the 1,888 always-detected, and the harm bias −0.0101 / −0.0806 / −0.0929 against −0.0102 / −0.0805 / −0.0915. In the series where detection moves most — the 31% null, 0.9205 → 0.9545 → 0.9590 — the harm field lower coverage is 0.9734 / 0.9560 / 0.9666 on all detected and 0.9745 / 0.9562 / 0.9686 on the 1,688 always-detected; the n-pattern is unchanged when composition is held fixed.

**What the screen removes is close to what it keeps, on the one pre-selection quantity the bundles record.** At the largest non-detection anywhere (12.4% HR 1.00 n500, 639 undetected) mean `n_true` is 61.43 undetected against 61.99 detected; in the 31% nulls it is 151.64 / 306.69 / 458.15 undetected against 153.07 / 306.23 / 459.75 detected. The one visible signal is at 12.4% HR 1.75 n500, where the 100 undetected replicates carry a smaller planted subgroup (`n_true` 57.61 against 62.04, realized prevalence 0.1152 against 0.1241) — the screen removes the smallest plants, about a 7% difference in n_true, on 5% of the replicates. H3b reads those same 100 replicates at n = 1000, where they are all detected: naive harm effect 1.8370 against 1.8660, naive SE 0.2299 against 0.2284, mean p̂ 0.3884 against 0.3736, IJ two-sided coverage 0.9500 against 0.9148. They are not a distinguishable population.

**Beside the p̂ composition shift of F4, detection conditioning is negligible — but the composition shift does not explain the decay either.** The 12.4% HR 1.75 series moves its mean p̂ 0.2337 → 0.3743 → 0.4979 and its [0.55,1.0] share 0.061 → 0.196 → 0.392 while the detected set changes by 5 percentage points and the always-detected restriction changes nothing. The composition shift is the large change; it runs toward the best-behaved band and would have raised the cell average, and the cell average fell. Neither conditioning is the mechanism; Part G's joint condition is.

**Limitation, stated for the record.** The template's `record_replicate()` returns the `.na_record()` at `status = "NO-DETECTION"`, so on an undetected replicate only `sim_id`, `detected`, `mr_ok`, `status`, `n_true`, `betaHhat_Hc`, `nH_eval`, `nHc_eval` and `fit_mr_secs` carry values. **The naive harm effect and its SE are not recorded for undetected replicates** — there is no Ĥ to fit — so H3's detected-vs-undetected comparison could be made on `n_true` (and the realized prevalence, β(Ĥᶜ) and `nHc_eval`) only. H3b is the substitute the committed bundles do support, and is labelled as such. Recording pre-selection quantities on non-detected replicates would need a template change and a re-run, both out of this task's scope.

**Pairing note.** Every cell ran with `seed_base = 8316951` over `sim_id` 1–2000, and `simulate_from_dgm()` draws its subjects as `sample(nrow(df_super), n, replace = TRUE)` under `set.seed(seed_base + sim_id)`, so within a series replicate *i* is the same seed at every n and its n = 500 subject draw is the leading block of its n = 1500 draw. The treatment allocation, drawn afterwards at the cell's own n, is not shared, and the selected Ĥ differs. The always-detected restriction therefore removes composition-by-detection; it does not make the replicates otherwise identical across n, which is exactly the comparison the task asks for.
