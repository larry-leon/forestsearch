## The recovery diagnostics

This is the first campaign-scale record of these metrics; they are descriptive and no
construction reads them.

`sens_H` — the mean share of the identified patients that the outer draws' re-selections retain
— rises with n on the harm rows: 0.399 [sd 0.149] / 0.498 [0.167] / 0.605 [0.170] at HR 1.50, and
0.373 [0.133] / 0.410 [0.142] on the two nulls. `ppv_H` tracks it almost exactly (0.406 / 0.496 /
0.605; correlation of the two within a cell 0.883-0.945). `sens_Hc` and `npv_Hc` are much higher
and move less (0.899 -> 0.945 across the harm rows).

The per-draw containment behind those means is wide: at HR 1.50 n = 500 the median replicate has
q10 = 0.067, q50 = 0.365, q90 = 0.795, and the share of draws whose re-selection contains Ĥ
exactly (`share1`) averages 0.120; by n = 1500 the same triple is 0.199 / 0.637 / 0.947 with
`share1` 0.331. `n_used` averages 959-994 of the 1,000 outer draws in every cell.

The metrics correlate more strongly with p̂(Ĥ) than with the truth-referenced sensitivity of Ĥ:
corr(`sens_H`, p̂) is 0.602 / 0.734 / 0.797 on the harm rows and 0.543 / 0.626 on the nulls,
against corr(`sens_H`, truth-referenced `sens`) of 0.357 / 0.433 / 0.406 and 0.190 / 0.253.
corr(`share1`, p̂) is higher still, 0.638-0.838.
