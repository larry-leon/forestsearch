### Per-cell wall-clock model for a 2,000-replicate cell

- `%dofuture%` (doFuture) with no `chunk.size` / `scheduling` option uses scheduling 1: `makeChunks()` → `splitIndices(n, W)`, one contiguous chunk per worker. A batch of 1,000 replicates ends when its slowest chunk ends.
- Validated on the anchors (100 workers, batch 2 in sim_id order), `fit_mr_secs`: max contiguous 100-chunk sum vs realized batch-2 `built_at` delta — A1 772 vs 915; A2 1207 vs 1315; A3 1943 vs 2043; A4 807 vs 941; A5 1249 vs 1345; A6 1977 vs 2053; A7 637 vs 799; A8 1042 vs 1194; A9 1717 vs 1862. The residual, 76–162 s, is taken as per-batch render overhead. (sum/100, for comparison: 727, 1148, 1884, 759, 1183, 1923, 573, 964, 1624.)
- Cell wall = 2 × batch (max over W contiguous chunks of 1,000 replicates drawn with replacement from the W run's 96 per-replicate values; B = 4000) + 2 × overhead (76 / 119 / 162 s) + combine render 18 s (driver smoke, n 500).
