**On the harm block the answer is no, and this is the finding of the section.** The harm field's T3 does not improve with n — 0.929 → 0.883 → 0.901 at HR 1.50 and 0.919 → 0.882 → 0.903 at HR 1.75 — it dips at n = 1000 and only partly recovers. More data does not resolve it because more data makes picks *more* stable, not less: mean p̂(Ĥ) rises with n (0.103 → 0.117 → 0.122 at HR 1.50; 0.119 → 0.144 → 0.168 at HR 1.75), so the stratum that under-covers is also the stratum that grows.

Both are the same stratum type -- the stable-pick third of the harm block, where the implied value is the more pessimistic of the two. Location plus scale continue to explain the shape on both blocks, at every n.
