## 1a — Implementation

**`R/fs_mr_inference.R`** — `field_complement = FALSE` (new last formal). Under `ci_method = "field"` the two `rnorm` matrices are now held (`Xo`, `Xi_f`) before projection — same two calls, same sizes, same order, so the stream and `Zo`/`Zi` are bit-identical to before. Under the flag (and only with `include_complement = TRUE`) the harm loop side-records each outer winner `G_out[r]` and the inner-winner row `W_in[r, ]` (NA where an inner draw had no winner) as it computes them; `lam`/`n_in_used` and every `field$*` output are untouched. After the harm field's list is assembled and *before* the uniform sweep, `.fs_mr_field_complement()` (new `@noRd` helper in the same file) fits lazily — same loop body as the gate's multiplier-stage complement fits, into the same `Bc`/`bh_c` cache — every candidate that wins on any outer or inner draw and was not already fit; projects the harm field's own ξ through the complement influence (`Zo_c = Bcᵀ Xo`, `Zi_c = Bcᵀ Xi_f`); forms `Λ*ᶜ_r = Zo_c[G_r, r] − mean_j Zi_c[win_{r,j}, j]` over inner draws with a winner whose complement is fit (outer draws dropped when the harm block dropped them, when `G_r`'s complement is unfit, or when no inner draw survives — counted as `n_out_dropped_unfit`); and inverts about `β̃ᶜ` (the gate's two-term de-biased complement estimate on the working scale, held as `bdc_w`). Returned as `field$complement`: `lambda_mean`, `lambda_sd`/`se_field`, the seven quantiles, `est2`, **`upper_1s` (primary, = β̃ᶜ − q₀.₀₅)**, `lower_1s`, `lower_2s`/`upper_2s`, `lower_se`/`upper_se`, `n_out_used`, `n_in_used_mean`, `n_out_dropped_unfit`, `n_complement_fits`, `n_new_fits`, `share_draws_new_fit` (share of outer + inner draw-winner readings whose candidate the multiplier stage had not fit), `R_out`, `R_in`, `timing_seconds`; a `note` list when the selected complement is unfit or fewer than 2 outer draws survive. The block draws nothing, so the uniform sweep's `+ 910000L` stream is unchanged. Roxygen states the orientation (benefit claim; exposed limit = upper bound; vulnerable to under-correction, immune to over-correction — the mirror of the harm side) and the `@return` shape; `man/fs_mr_inference.Rd` regenerated.
