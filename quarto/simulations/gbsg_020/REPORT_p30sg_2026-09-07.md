## Reading

By location and coverage, never as significance at 1.0. **Identification.** `effMaxSG` does what its rule says — it takes the largest candidate within 10% of the maximal effect — and that enlarges Ĥ by 18–29% on average (94–99 vs 80 at n = 500; 170 vs 132 at n = 1000), never shrinks it, lifts sensitivity by 5–11 points and trims the naive optimism on Ĥ by 0.3–0.6 SD; but the candidates in the band are still sub-regions of the planted one (median |Ĥ|/|H| 0.58–0.62), because the `er ≤ 59` boundary is not representable on the search's grid and the consistency screen admits the same family under both foci. Capture of the planted region is a property of the family, not of the selection functional applied to it. **Constructions.** On Ĥ the picture is p30's: field lower bounds at 0.50–0.84 with one-sided coverage 0.946–0.971 (two-sided 0.932–0.985), IJ two-term within 0.02 of the field's location at 0.97–0.98 coverage, the winner-only IJ under-covering at 0.66–0.77 (r = 0.59–0.71) — the harm side remains the regime where the winner-only residual fails. On Ĥᶜ the regime has started to move in the harm cells (SD(β̃ᶜ)/naive SE 1.08 and 1.12), and the constructions respond as the task anticipated: the winner-only SE (r ≈ 0.8) covers the upper bound at 0.87–0.88, the field — whose λ-SDᶜ still equals the naive SE (0.97×) — at 0.91–0.92, 1–2 points under its p30 values and just under the 0.92 floor, while the IJ two-term's doubled SE covers at 0.996 with an upper bound at 1.22–1.25 that rules out nothing. Bound locations: field upper bounds 1.02 / 1.04 in the harm cells against β(Ĥᶜ) = 0.84 / 0.85 (below 0.85 on 11–13% of replicates), 0.94 / 0.88 in the null cells against 0.77; the joint Bonferroni pair holds at 0.938–0.942 (Wilson upper limits 0.948–0.952 — at the edge of Monte Carlo error, one point under p30's 0.939–0.949) with the calibrated pair coinciding (corr +0.03 to +0.07) and the separate bounds at 0.88. **What this says.** The complement's selection variability becomes visible only when the *realized* Ĥ grows, and `effMaxSG` grows it by a fifth, not to the planted size; at that point the field's complement SE (the naive SE, in this regime) is already 1–2 coverage points short, which is the first sign of the failure mode the 12.5% designs could not show. A design in which the planted boundary is on the grid (or forced) would let the identifier reach |H| and put the constructions where the complement's variability is fully expressed — noted, not proposed.
