**Reading of block 1.** **The band effect (arm A vs p30sg, J = 10 fixed):** widening ε from 0.10 to 0.20 enlarges Ĥ from 97 / 99 to 127 / 129 (mean |Ĥ|/|H| 0.64 → 0.83 and 0.65 → 0.85; median 0.60 → 0.79, 0.62 → 0.82), lifts sensitivity from 0.45 / 0.51 to 0.59 / 0.66 and NPV by 4–5 points, at a cost of 1.7–2.4 points of specificity; PPV is unchanged (0.70–0.71, 0.77–0.78). On 79% of replicates the wider band moves the pick to a larger candidate and on none to a smaller one (the J = 10 family is the same). The share of replicates with |Ĥ| ≥ 0.9|H| rises from 0.11 / 0.14 to 0.39 / 0.43 — the planted size is now reached on two-fifths of replicates — and the 90th percentile of |Ĥ|/|H| is **1.18 / 1.16: the wider band overshoots the planted region on the upper tail** (Ĥ larger than H, with the lost specificity), which ε = 0.10 never did (q90 0.93 / 0.95). β(Ĥ) is unchanged (1.256 vs 1.252; 1.515 vs 1.512) — the extra members are, on average, as harmed as the old ones — while β(Ĥᶜ) moves 0.03–0.04 closer to θ†(Hᶜ) (0.808 vs 0.835 / 0.848), and the naive optimism on Ĥ falls by 0.7 SD (+1.81 vs +2.48; +1.48 vs +2.12). **The grid effect at the chosen band (arm B vs arm A):** J = 20 changes almost nothing on average — mean |Ĥ| 124 / 126 vs 127 / 129, sensitivity 0.58 / 0.65 vs 0.59 / 0.66, the same β(Ĥ), β(Ĥᶜ) and optimism — but it re-picks: on the two harm cells only 25–30% of replicates keep the same rule, and Ĥ is larger on 35–37%, smaller on 33–36%. The finer grid offers the `er ≤ 50` cut (95% of the truth's er-mass) but the band already reached the region's size on the J = 10 grid; the consistency-qualifying set grows ×1.55 (202 → 314; 277 → 431) and the band ×1.2 (10 → 13), so the selection is drawn from more near-ties, not from a better-placed boundary. **The null cells under arm B:** Ĥ grows from 94 to 116 at n = 500 and from 170 to 238 at n = 1000 (|Ĥ|/|H| 0.62 → 0.76, 0.56 → 0.78), sensitivity 0.30 → 0.38 and 0.32 → 0.45, specificity down 3–4 points, detection up 2 points (the larger family); the overshoot appears here too (q90 1.10 / 1.18). **Regime:** p̂(Ĥ) is 0.07–0.12 on average (median 0.05–0.08) with the selected candidate the re-selection argmax on only 21–28% of replicates and the top-3 holding 0.25–0.40 of the mass — the competitive regime the p30sg Stage 1 fits anticipated, now measured on 2,000 replicates per cell.

The J = 20 grid adds candidates (K ×1.65) and re-draws the pick on three replicates in four without moving any mean: at this band the boundary's representability is no longer what limits capture; the selection among many near-tied candidates is (p̂(Ĥ) ≈ 0.05–0.08 at the median, argmax on a quarter of replicates). **Constructions.** On Ĥ the field's one-sided lower bound holds at 0.956–0.976 through a one-third larger region and a two-thirds larger family — the harm side is robust to both knobs; the IJ two-term stays at 0.98 with a 0.06–0.10 wider margin. On Ĥᶜ the regime keeps moving as Ĥ grows: SD(β̃ᶜ)/naive SE 1.10–1.15 at n = 500 and 1.20 at harm 1.5 n1000, while the field's λ-SDᶜ stays at 0.95 of the naive SE, so its upper-bound coverage sits at 0.90–0.91 in the n = 500 harm cells (1–2 points under p30sg, 2–3 under the floor) and recovers to 0.938 at n = 1000 where the region is captured more exactly (|Ĥ|/|H| 0.86) — the coverage shortfall tracks the selection variability of the complement, not its size; the IJ two-term covers at 0.995+ with a bound that rules out nothing (1.04–1.21).
