Detection, bias, coverage, and CI width vs sample size

Figure 1 shows, for the MR de-biased gate and the naive estimator across sample sizes and detectors: subgroup detection, estimator bias relative to the target, 95% CI coverage, and median CI width. In the 2×2 layout the panels are (A) detection, (B) bias, (C) coverage, (D) width; the standalone figures below render only when the layout leaves them separate.

Estimator bias vs sample size

?@fig-mr-bias compares the bias of each estimator relative to the target (target_pick) on the chosen scale; a value at 0 means unbiased. The oracle line is the sanity reference (near 0); the naive line carries the selection bias the MR gate is meant to remove.

Width of the 95% CIs vs sample size

?@fig-mr-cilength reports the median 95% CI width across sample sizes for each detector and estimator — the precision companion to the coverage curve.
