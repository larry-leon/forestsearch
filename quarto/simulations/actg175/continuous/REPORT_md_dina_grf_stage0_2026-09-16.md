## 3. S0.2 — Orientation and floors

**Claims.**
- *"DINA's proposal floor is applied to the raw surface with no adverse-outcome orientation, so under `adverse_outcome = FALSE` its proposal floor and its re-ranking floor point in opposite directions."* — **Confirmed.** `dina()` is fit on the raw outcome (`helpers:1403–1406`: `fit_call <- c(list(df = df, outcome = outcome.name, treatment = treat.name, ...), da$fit)`; no flip); its surface is `tau_hat <- as.numeric(beta[1L] + X %*% beta[-1L])` (`R/dina_subgroup.R:412`), the raw-Y treatment effect (higher `cd4_change` is better, so harm is negative there); candidates must satisfy `if (mean_tau < m_diff) next` (`:726`, `:865`) with `m_diff = hr.threshold` for gaussian (`helpers:1411`). The re-ranking (`select_statistic = "effect"`, the default) scores each qualifying candidate with `.fs_mr_pieces()` under `adverse_outcome` (`helpers:1178–1182`, `:1205`), i.e. on the **negated** Y (`R/consistency_resample.R:255–257`), and admits `eff_link[scorable] >= floor_cmp` with `floor_cmp = admission$effect_floor` (`:1222–1224`), where `effect_floor <- as.numeric(hr.threshold)` (`:2345`; DINA and GRF: `c(effect = TRUE, consistency = FALSE)`, `:2282–2283`). Under `adverse_outcome = FALSE` the proposal floor reads raw MD ≥ +30 (a **benefit** of 30) and the re-ranking floor reads harm-oriented MD ≥ +30 (a **harm** of 30): opposite directions. Observed on sim_id 1 (§4): 8,324 candidates searched, 3 qualify on the raw floor, 0 admitted on the harm floor, no subgroup.
- *"DINA's continuous floor is `hr.threshold` itself (default 1.25), while FS screens at MD > 0."* — **Differs in both halves.** The floor is `hr.threshold` (`:1411`), but `hr.threshold` is aliased from `effect.threshold` whenever that is passed (`main:1352` `if (!is.null(effect.threshold)) hr.threshold <- effect.threshold`); the MD template passes `effect.threshold = 30`, so the floor is 30, not 1.25 (1.25 applies only when neither is passed, and is then remapped to 0 for MD: `:1850–1852`). FS screens at `effect_threshold <- hr.threshold` = 30 on the harm-oriented MD (`:1793`; the run's own banner: "Screening: MD >= 30.0000; Consistency: MD >= 10.0000"), not at MD > 0.
