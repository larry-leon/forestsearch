## 1. The design, in one block

- ACTG175 (arms 1 and 3, `speff2trial::ACTG175`), outcome `cd4_change = cd420 − cd40` (continuous; MD), treatment ddI = 1; `adverse_outcome = FALSE`, so the identifier and the MR gate work on −`cd4_change` and every recorded estimate, bound and threshold is on the **harm-oriented MD scale (positive = harm)**; `betaHhat_*` in the bundles are raw and are oriented with −1 by every summary.
- One template: `sim_fs_maxeffCons_mr_field_md_template.qmd` (`FS_MD_*` knobs; focus and band knobs `FS_MD_FOCUS` / `FS_MD_NBHD`, complement-scale knob `FS_MD_FIELD_SCALEC`, campaign tag `FS_MD_CAMPAIGN`; every committed bundle path is refused by `.refuse_if_tracked()`).
- Cells: planted harm-region MD 40 at n = 500 and n = 700, MD 120 at n = 500 (direct build at the locked `k_inter`), and the **null** cell at n = 500 (no treatment-by-subgroup interaction; a homogeneous −26.26 raw, +26.26 harm-oriented, everywhere). True region `age > 34 & preanti <= 744.5`, prevalence 0.345; complement effect −26.26 raw in every cell. 2,000 replicates per cell.
- Seeds are `8316951 + sim_id` throughout (L'Ecuyer-CMRG), so the two campaigns share every DGM draw cell for cell: `n_true` identical and the oracle columns equal within 1e-8 on all 2,000 sim_ids (checked in both directions by `scripts_mdsgnb20/gate2.R`).
- Identification: consistency engine, `consistency_method = "resample"`, effect threshold 30 and consistency threshold 10 (MD scale), `pconsistency.threshold` 0.90, `fs.splits` 400, `maxk` 2, `n.min` 60, arm minima 12 / 12, J = 10 cut grids on `age` and `preanti`, `str2` in the pool. MR: 5,000 multiplier draws, `ci_method = "field"`, field R_out / R_in 1000 / 500, IJ two-term, `return_reselection = TRUE`.

## 4. Reading conventions that must travel with these numbers

- **Scale.** Everything is harm-oriented MD: positive = harm, in CD4 cells/mm³. A raw `cd4_change` figure is the sign flip.
- **Bound location, never significance.** Ĥ: the one-sided 95% lower bound ≥ τ reads "harm of at least τ supported"; Ĥᶜ: the one-sided 95% upper bound ≤ τ reads "harm of at most τ supported"; the ladder is τ = 0, 10, 20, 30, 40, 60, 80, 100 (0 = no harm, 10 = the consistency threshold, 30 = the effect threshold, 40 = the planted MD in the md40 cells). No result is framed as significance at the null.
- **The null cell's truth** is no subgroup and a homogeneous +26 for every patient, so β(Ĥ) = β(Ĥᶜ) = +26.26 for every declared Ĥ there. Its declaration rate stands beside it in every table; its bound locations are read against +26, not as a false-claim rate.
- **Coverage** is of the exact super-population targets β(Ĥ), β(Ĥᶜ) at the realized rule, conditional on declaration; the oracle is scored against the structural true-region effect and is blank on Ĥ in the null cell. Wilson intervals and Monte Carlo SEs on every rate.
- **Conditional on the proposed family (`mdgrf`, `mddina`).** GRF's and DINA's candidate families are generated from fitted surfaces, so the fixed-family condition does not hold for them: every `mdgrf` and `mddina` coverage figure is coverage of the estimand conditional on the proposed family, and every comparison across FS, GRF and DINA is descriptive (identifier, family construction and detection set differ). FS's family is the prespecified cut grid. On DINA, `dina_proposed_n`, `dina_searched_n`, `dina_tau_min` and `admitted_n` exist on declared replicates only. The true-positive count is sensitivity × `n_true`, never |Ĥ| (the recorder's `n_harm` is |Ĥ|).
- **Timing.** `fit_mr_secs` contains `fld_H_secs` and `fld_Hc_secs`; nested timing columns are never summed.
- **Excluded everywhere:** IJ winner-only and winner-floor, the κ (uniform) variants, `field_uniform`; FB rows (FB was off in `mdsgnb20`).
- Reported marginal SDs carry the target's own spread across replicates (β(Ĥ) moves with Ĥ); they are not error SDs.

## 5. Superseded — do not quote as the current product

- **`mdf1`'s unstudentized complement field bound (`fld_Hc_up1s`) as the reported complement product.** Superseded by the studentized complement field (field-s, `fld_Hc_up1s_s`), the gate's default construction since `fb62705c` and the evaluated Ĥᶜ row in `mdsgnb20`. `mdf1` predates field-s (its recorder has no `_s` column); its complement figures are kept as the paired before/after in the `mdsgnb20` summary, not as a product.
- `mdf1`'s rule (`maxeffCons`) is not the survival grid's; its Ĥ-block figures are read beside `mdsgnb20`'s in the paired rule contrast, not as a replacement for them.
